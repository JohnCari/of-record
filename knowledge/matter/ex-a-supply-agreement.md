# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Every party, person, date and amount in this matter is fictional and was
> written for a software demonstration. It is not a real contract and not legal advice.

This Master Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of June 3, 2024 (the "Effective Date") between Cottonwood Gulch Fabrication LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("Seller"), and Tumbleweed Ridge Outfitters Inc., a Colorado corporation ("Buyer").

## 1. Goods

1.1 Seller shall manufacture and sell to Buyer powder-coated steel rack frames and related hardware (the "Goods") as described in purchase orders issued by Buyer and accepted by Seller.

1.2 Each accepted purchase order is governed by this Agreement. If a purchase order conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls.

## 2. Delivery

2.1 Seller shall deliver the Goods to Buyer's warehouse at 4400 Quarry Spur Road, Aurora, Colorado. Title and risk of loss pass to Buyer on delivery.

2.2 Buyer shall sign a delivery receipt for each shipment. A signed delivery receipt is conclusive evidence of the quantity delivered.

## 3. Inspection and Rejection

3.1 Buyer shall inspect the Goods within ten (10) business days after delivery (the "Inspection Period").

3.2 Buyer may reject nonconforming Goods only by written notice to Seller, delivered within the Inspection Period, that describes the nonconformity with reasonable particularity.

3.3 Goods not rejected in accordance with Section 3.2 are deemed accepted at the end of the Inspection Period.

3.4 Seller shall, at its option, repair or replace Goods that are timely rejected. This is Buyer's exclusive remedy for nonconforming Goods that are timely rejected.

## 4. Price

4.1 The price of the Goods is the price stated in the accepted purchase order.

## 5. Payment

5.1 Seller shall invoice Buyer on or after delivery of each shipment.

5.2 Buyer shall pay each invoice in full within thirty (30) days after the invoice date.

5.3 Any amount not paid when due accrues interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month from the due date until paid.

5.4 Buyer shall not set off or withhold any amount owed to Seller under this Agreement on account of any claim against Seller unless Seller has agreed to the setoff in writing.

## 6. Notices

6.1 A notice under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by email to the address below, with a copy by overnight courier.

6.2 Notices to Seller: accounts@cottonwoodgulch.example. Notices to Buyer: purchasing@tumbleweedridge.example.

## 7. Attorney Fees

7.1 In any action to enforce this Agreement, the prevailing party is entitled to recover its reasonable attorney fees and costs.

## 8. General

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.

8.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties concerning its subject matter and may be amended only by a writing signed by both parties.

8.3 No waiver of any provision is effective unless in a writing signed by the waiving party.
